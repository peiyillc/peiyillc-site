Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Payment is processed securely by Stripe. You can pay with major credit and debit cards and other methods Stripe offers in your region. PEIYI LLC never sees or stores your full card details.

How does the weekly subscription work?

Click Subscribe and you go straight to secure checkout. You are billed $9.00 USD automatically every week through Stripe. Each week covers teaching on bean selection, roasting methods, and brewing technique. You can cancel anytime by emailing info@peiyillc.com and we will cancel it for you.

When do I choose my session time?

At checkout you'll share your preferred dates and times along with your time zone. We'll email you from info@peiyillc.com within 1 business day to confirm a time.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. You can reschedule once at no charge if you let us know at least 24 hours before your session.

What is your refund policy?

Cancel at least 24 hours before your session for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours and missed sessions are not refundable. If we can't find a mutually workable time within 14 days of your purchase, we'll refund you in full. See the full Refund Policy.

Do I need special equipment?

No. We work with whatever you have, from a simple French press to a home espresso setup. A kitchen scale helps but isn't required.

Is the session recorded?

No. Instead, you receive a written brew guide after the session so you have everything you need.

Can I buy a session as a gift?

Yes. Complete checkout and, in the preferred times field, note that it's a gift along with the recipient's name. We'll arrange the details with you by email.
