Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE OF LAUNCH]

This policy explains how PEIYI LLC ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit peiyillc.com or book a session.

Information we collect

  • Booking information: your name, email address, billing country or address, preferred session times, session focus and equipment details you provide at checkout.
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details (such as card brand and last four digits) but never your full card number.
  • Messages: information you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability.

How we use it

To schedule and deliver your session, send your brew guide, process payments and refunds, answer your questions, and meet legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use Stripe (payments), our website host (hosting and contact forms), our email provider, and a video-call platform to deliver sessions. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

Retention

We keep booking and payment records for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, and other messages only as long as needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing info@peiyillc.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe may use cookies on its checkout page for security and fraud prevention.
