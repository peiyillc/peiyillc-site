About us

Good coffee shouldn't be a guessing game

PEIYI LLC is a coffee consulting company founded in 2026. We help coffee lovers make better decisions about what they buy and how they brew.

Why we exist

Specialty coffee has never been easier to buy, yet many people still end up with cups that taste sour, bitter or simply disappointing. Labels are full of unfamiliar terms, brewing advice online often contradicts itself, and it's hard to know which variable to change first.

We started PEIYI LLC to make that simpler. In one focused conversation, we translate coffee jargon into practical choices and give you a method you can repeat at home.

Our approach

  • Personal. Every session starts from your taste, your budget and the equipment you already own.
  • Practical. You leave with specific recipes and a short buying list, not just theory.
  • Clear. We explain the "why" behind each adjustment so you can keep improving on your own.
  • Honest. If something you own is holding you back, we'll tell you, and if it isn't, we'll say that too.
